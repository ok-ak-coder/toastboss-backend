J. Kent Hastings

Member since 2026

What made you want to join I'll Drink to That?

To improve business presentations.

How has being part of this club helped you?

It's given me 'stage time' and instruction on timing, structure and vocal variety.

What keeps you coming back?

The opportunity to act in different roles as well as speaking.

What is your favorite thing about our club?

More levity than a typical business club.

Tell us a little about yourself outside of Toastmasters.

I'm a web developer and ham radio operator.

What is something people might be surprised to learn about you?

I edited video for a gun school and for three low-budget indie feature films.

Is there anything else you would like to add?

I'd like to add a few million dollars to my net worth.
