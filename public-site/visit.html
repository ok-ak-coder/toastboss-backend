Directions & Meeting Location

Come see for yourself. Guests are always welcome, there's no obligation to speak, and you can just watch the first time if you'd rather ease in. Here's where to find us Thursday night.

View Our Meeting Schedule Read the FAQs

Rum Runner on Tropicana

I'll Drink to That Toastmasters meets in the Lombardi Room at Rum Runner, 1801 E. Tropicana Ave., Las Vegas, NV 89119, near Tropicana and Spencer.

The meeting room is just to the right of the entrance to the Badger Cafe. You can enter through the cafe or use the separate entrance directly from the sidewalk.

Open Rum Runner in Google Maps

Coming From I-15

I-15 Northbound

Take the Tropicana Avenue exit, head east on Tropicana, and continue to Rum Runner near Spencer Street.

I-15 Southbound

Take the Tropicana Avenue exit, head east on Tropicana, and continue to Rum Runner near Spencer Street.

Coming From US-95

US-95 Northbound

Take the Tropicana Avenue exit and turn left onto Tropicana, heading west. Continue west to 1801 E. Tropicana Ave., near Spencer Street.

US-95 Southbound

Take the Tropicana Avenue exit and turn right onto Tropicana, heading west. Continue west to 1801 E. Tropicana Ave., near Spencer Street.

Thursday meetings begin at 6:30 PM. Many members arrive early, so feel free to come a little ahead of time.
