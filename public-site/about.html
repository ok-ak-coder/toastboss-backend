A Different Kind of Las Vegas Toastmasters Club

I'll Drink to That Toastmasters is a social Las Vegas Toastmasters club focused on public speaking, confidence, communication, and connection.

Our members come from all kinds of backgrounds and experience levels. Some join to become stronger speakers, some want to feel more comfortable speaking in professional or social settings, and others are looking for a place to keep challenging themselves while meeting interesting people.

What to Expect

Our meetings are structured, interactive, and easy to follow. Members give prepared speeches, practice impromptu speaking through Barroom Topics, and support one another with thoughtful feedback.

We meet at Rum Runner, and many members arrive up to 30 minutes early to socialize, order food or drinks, and catch up before the meeting begins. That time before the meeting is part of the experience, and it gives visitors a chance to meet people before things get started.

Barroom Topics

Barroom Topics is I'll Drink to That's name for Toastmasters Table Topics, the impromptu speaking portion of the meeting.

Members are given a question or topic and respond on the spot, usually for a minute or two. It is a chance to practice thinking quickly, organizing your thoughts, and speaking without preparation.

It is also one of the parts of the meeting where the personality of the club really comes through.

The Social Side of I'll Drink to That

The social side of I'll Drink to That is a big part of what makes the club what it is.

Members spend time talking before meetings, getting to know one another, sharing stories, joking around, and building friendships over time. The room is full of familiar faces, different personalities, and people who genuinely enjoy seeing each other each week.

That sense of connection is part of the reason I'll Drink to That has continued to be a lasting part of the Las Vegas Toastmasters community.

Part of Las Vegas Toastmasters Since 1977

I'll Drink to That has been part of the Las Vegas Toastmasters community since 1977.

Over the years, the members have changed, the meetings have evolved, and new generations of speakers have come through the club. The purpose has remained the same: give people a place to practice speaking, build confidence, connect with others, and keep improving.

Come Meet Us

The best way to get a feel for I'll Drink to That is to visit a meeting.

Guests are always welcome, visiting is free, and there is no pressure to join. You are welcome to participate, or simply watch and get a sense of how the club works.

Come a little early, meet a few people, grab something to eat or drink if you like, and spend a Thursday evening with one of Las Vegas's longest-running Toastmasters clubs.
