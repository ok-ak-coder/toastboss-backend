Visiting & Guests

Can I visit before joining?

Absolutely. Visitors are always welcome, and there is no obligation to join. Come see what a meeting is like, meet the group, and participate only as much as you feel comfortable.

Do I need to RSVP, bring anything, or prepare ahead of time?

No. You can simply show up. There is nothing to prepare and nothing special you need to bring.

Do I have to speak at my first meeting?

No. Guests are welcome to simply watch. If you want to participate, you can, but nobody is going to force you to give a speech or put you on the spot.

Can I bring a friend?

Of course. Friends, coworkers, partners, and other guests are welcome.

Is there a dress code?

No. Our meetings are casual. Come in whatever you would normally feel comfortable wearing to a neighborhood bar or casual gathering.

Do I have to drink alcohol?

Not at all. We meet inside Rum Runner Lounge, but drinking is completely optional.

Is parking available?

Yes. Free parking is available at Rum Runner.

Meetings & The Club

What is Toastmasters?

Toastmasters is an international organization built around learning communication and leadership skills through practice. Instead of sitting through classes or lectures, members learn by speaking, taking meeting roles, giving feedback, and participating regularly. Leadership grows the same way, by running meetings, leading projects, and eventually taking on club officer roles like President or VP of Education if you want to.

What happens during a typical meeting?

Most meetings include prepared speeches, impromptu speaking, evaluations, and rotating meeting roles. The atmosphere is structured enough to help you improve, but relaxed enough to have fun while doing it.

What are Barroom Topics?

Barroom Topics are our club's variation on traditional Toastmasters Table Topics. They are short, impromptu speaking exercises built around interesting, funny, or thought-provoking questions.

How is I'll Drink to That different from other Toastmasters clubs?

We've been part of the Las Vegas Toastmasters community since 1977. We meet in a bar rather than a classroom or conference room, and our meetings tend to have a relaxed, social atmosphere while still giving members serious opportunities to improve their speaking and communication skills.

Where and when do you meet?

We meet Thursdays at 6:30 PM in the Lombardi Room at Rum Runner Lounge, 1801 E. Tropicana Ave. Meetings usually finish around 8:00 PM.

Are meetings available online?

Not right now, and that's on purpose. We want meetings to feel like a real social gathering, not a video call. Being in the same room, face to face, is a big part of what makes the club fun and what makes the speaking practice actually stick. If you're looking for real connection along with the skill-building, that's exactly what in-person meetings give you.

Membership

How much does it cost to join?

Toastmasters International membership dues are $72 every six months. Our club dues are $12 every six months. New members pay a one-time new member fee of $25 when joining, which includes your first Pathway.

Contact us for the current total before joining, since dues are prorated.

Can I attend a few meetings before deciding whether to join?

Yes. In fact, we encourage it. Visit, see how the club feels, and decide whether it is something you would enjoy being part of.

How often do members have to give speeches?

There is no fixed speaking quota. Members progress at their own pace. You can volunteer for speeches and meeting roles based on your own goals and availability.

What kind of support do new members receive?

Members receive constructive evaluations, help from experienced club members, and access to Toastmasters educational resources. You do not have to figure everything out on your own.

What is Pathways?

Pathways is Toastmasters International's educational program. It provides structured speaking and leadership projects that members complete over time.

Is Toastmasters Right for Me?

What if I'm afraid of public speaking?

That's one of the most common reasons people come to Toastmasters. You do not have to already be confident or polished. The whole point is having a place where you can practice without the pressure of needing to be perfect.

Can Toastmasters help outside of formal public speaking?

Definitely. Members use the skills they practice here for work presentations, interviews, meetings, storytelling, leadership, everyday conversations, and simply becoming more comfortable speaking in front of other people.

What if English isn't my first language?

You're welcome here. Toastmasters clubs include people from many backgrounds and experience levels, and regular speaking practice can be especially useful for people who want to become more comfortable communicating in English.
