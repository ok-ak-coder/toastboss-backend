Club Treasurer · Member since 1984

I joined I'll Drink to That Toastmasters in October of 1984. Throughout my professional life, the speaking and leadership skills I learned in Toastmasters helped me be successful in any endeavor I sought to accomplish. The friends and acquaintances I have made due to my Toastmaster involvement continue to enrich my life even now. I look forward to sharing my Toastmaster experience.
