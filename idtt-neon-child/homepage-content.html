I'll Drink to That!

Las Vegas · Since 1977

I'll Drink to That is the Toastmasters club that meets at a bar, and yes, it's as relaxed as it sounds. Since 1977, we've been helping people in Las Vegas get more comfortable speaking in front of a room, making us one of the city's longest-running Toastmasters clubs.

Beginners are welcome, and so are people who just want to check us out. Jump into Barroom Topics if you feel like it, or simply sit back and watch. There's no pressure to join, and you can visit as many times as you like.

At heart, we're a social club. We meet at the Rum Runner to practice speaking, meet interesting people, and build real friendships without making the whole thing feel like a seminar. Public speaking is a lot more fun when the room feels like a hangout, not a boardroom.

More than public speaking

See what you can do.

Build confidence, develop leadership skills, meet new people, and discover strengths you may never have known you had.

Find Your Voice

Build confidence speaking in front of a room, organize your thoughts, and get more comfortable expressing your ideas clearly.

Grow as a Leader

Take on meeting roles, give feedback, mentor others, organize, facilitate, and practice leadership in a supportive environment.

Discover New Strengths

Try new roles, experiment, and hone skills you may never have known you had. You might surprise yourself.

Learn by Doing

Prepared speeches, impromptu speaking, evaluations, and meeting roles give you real opportunities to practice and improve.

Meet Your People

Build camaraderie, make new contacts, and spend time with interesting people who are here to learn, grow, and have a good time.

Think on Your Feet

Develop quick thinking, creativity, flexibility, and the confidence to handle the unexpected when you are put on the spot.

Come see for yourself

Pull up a seat

No sign up, no pressure, no obligation to speak a word. Guests sit, sip, and watch the first time. Most come back and try it themselves the second.
